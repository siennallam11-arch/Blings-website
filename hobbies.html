Hobbies

Pickleball

I’ve played tennis my whole life, but over the past year and a half I’ve gotten serious about pickleball.

Today, I serve on the executive team of Pickleball at Berkeley, UC Berkeley’s collegiate team. We compete on the intercollegiate tour and made it to Nationals in 2026, and I’ve also won two intramural championships at Berkeley.

In my free time, I love competing, traveling around the Bay Area to play, meeting new people on the court, and teaching lessons.
